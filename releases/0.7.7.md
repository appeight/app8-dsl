Patch release on 0.7.6. All SDKs move to 0.7.7 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1.

- Top bar scroll-edge effect ([navigation.md](../docs/navigation.md), `navigationBar.topEdgeEffect`):
  - **iOS 26:** the effect is UIKit's own `UIScrollEdgeEffect`, exactly as a stock UIKit navigation bar draws it. The value maps one to one: `automatic` (the default, no longer forced to `soft`), `soft` and `hard` are the system styles, and `hidden` hides the effect. Removed:
    - the App8-drawn overlay from 0.7.4 and 0.7.5, with its tint in the screen's background colour;
    - the hard band and hairline;
    - the fade-in that waited for content to reach the bar;
    - the content sampler that turned it dark or light.
  - **iOS before 26:** unchanged, the system's default bar appearance.
  - **Web renderer, iOS mode:** a pure progressive backdrop blur with no colour, tint or hairline.
  - **Android:** unchanged.

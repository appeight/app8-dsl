Patch release on 0.7.10. All SDKs move to 0.7.11 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1. Every change is backward compatible: new keys are ignored by older engines, which draw what they drew before.

- Engine-drawn tab bars ([tab-bar-screen.md](../docs/components/tab-bar-screen.md#flat-bars)): with a non-glass `barStyle.background`, the engine draws the bar on every platform with an exact `height` (at least 28). `shape: "floating"` (default) keeps the iOS 26 placement (inset from the sides, above the bottom safe area) with `cornerRadius` corners (`0` a rectangle, default a capsule) and an optional `bottomOffset` from the bottom safe area; `shape: "edge"` is a full-width stripe flush with the bottom edge. `badgePlacement: "inline"` puts badges on the label's line. Glass bars stay the platform bar.
- Stretchy top ([scroll-view.md](../docs/components/scroll-view.md#stretchy-top)): the pull-down at the top scales the hero (the first child, descending into a first-child container) uniformly about its bottom edge, keeping its aspect ratio, on iOS, Android and the web renderer. Before, iOS moved the first child's constraints (a stack moved with the pull, so nothing grew and the pull looked blocked) and the web renderer scaled the whole content; Android had no effect.
- `contentInsetAdjustment: "bottom"` ([scroll-view.md](../docs/components/scroll-view.md#contentinsetadjustment-values)): content starts under the top bars, the bottom inset follows the safe area. For full-bleed hero screens. Engines up to 0.7.10 read it as `automatic`.
- Bottom bar inset ([navigation.md](../docs/navigation.md#layout-1)): the screen's main vertical scroll view gets the bottom bar's height as bottom inset even with `contentInsetAdjustment: "never"`.
- Context menu ([context-menu.md](../docs/context-menu.md)): `preview.cornerRadius` (`0` for square brands). The preview fits its content's height without stretched gaps and appears in one piece (no doubled frame). Tapping the preview opens the destination as the preview expands, with no second transition after it.
- iOS zoom from a full-screen source ([transitions.md](../docs/transitions.md#native-zoom-from-a-source-element)): a source covering at least 80% of the screen (a full-screen pager page) is an engine-drawn push: the photo flies into the destination's `alignmentKey` element while the new screen fades in at full size, the presenting screen never scales or dims; drag to dismiss turns the screen into a card under the finger that flies back onto the photo. Before, the system zoom shrank and dimmed the presenting screen and showed the new screen at once.

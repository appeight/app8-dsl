Patch release on 0.5.1 (spec: https://github.com/appeight/app8-dsl/releases/tag/0.5.0). Fixes from device testing of the DemoStore demo. All SDKs move to 0.5.2 together.
- `navigationBar.largeTitle`: large title in the navigation bar that collapses as the screen's list scrolls (iOS; Android uses `topAppBar: "large"`).
- Self-sizing grid rows: without `itemHeight`/`aspectRatio`, grid items measure at their smallest natural height and each row takes its tallest item (iOS no longer stretches every item to `estimatedItemHeight`).
- Collection sections follow every variable their `data` expression reads (iOS updated only on the first identifier, so filtered lists ignored changes).
- iOS: menu `picker` no longer crashes when its options become empty.
- iOS: line-limited labels keep their tail ellipsis when a `lineHeight` is set; system button titles honour `numberOfLines: 1`.
- iOS: zoom-pushed screens let a back drag that starts over a pager on its first page pop; the navigation bar no longer jumps after push/pop from a `fillsBar` search screen; a list laid out off screen rests below the bars when first shown.

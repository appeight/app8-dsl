Patch release on 0.6.1, which is identical except that its Android engine package did not publish (API dump check). All SDKs move to 0.6.2 together.

- `requestNotificationPermission` action with `onGranted` / `onDenied`: asks for push notification permission (iOS `UNUserNotificationCenter`, Android 13+ `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`).
- `navigationBar.search.mode: "button"` with `onTap`: the bar shows the real search field but a tap runs actions instead of focusing, so it still morphs into the back button or into the next screen's search field. Also `autoFocus`, `onCancel`, and `navigationBar.hidesBackButton`.
- Tapping the selected tab again pops to its root, or scrolls its main vertical scroller to the top (iOS, Android, web renderer).
- Every vertical scroll view bounces (iOS `alwaysBounceVertical`; Android stretch even when content fits) unless `bounces: false`.
- iOS: self-sizing grid rows keep their measured heights when items scroll off screen (height cache by item identity), so scrolling tracks the finger, nothing jumps after deceleration and the bottom overscroll stays smooth. Cells apply bound text and visibility before they are measured; computed variables recompute when a reused cell gets a new item.
- iOS: the tab bar slides in with an interactive pop and back out when it is cancelled. A search field filling the bar stays inside the bar margins when focused and with trailing items.
- Android: leaving a screen dismisses the keyboard of a focused search field.

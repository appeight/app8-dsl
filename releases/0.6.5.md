Patch release on 0.6.4. All SDKs move to 0.6.5 together.
- iOS and Android: a material shadow no longer paints its shape in the shadow color, and shadows always draw under fills, whatever their order in the material list. A capsule with a translucent fill and a soft shadow now renders like the web renderer instead of as a solid dark shape.
- iOS: bar buttons take the navigation bar tint.
- iOS: search. A `mode: "button"` field never stays active and runs `onTap` once per tap; `autoFocus` retries while the screen is on top; a pushed search screen keeps room for its back button.
- iOS: self-sizing collection and table rows measure multi-line labels at their wrapped height, so a row no longer clips to its first line.
- iOS: the tab bar screen runs navigation requests in order, each after the running push or pop finishes, so `[navigation back toRoot, selectTab]` from a screen with `hidesTabBar` no longer overlaps two transitions.
- Spec: navigation docs. Search screens keep the back button and show start content so they never look empty.

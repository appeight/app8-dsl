Patch release on 0.6.5. All SDKs move to 0.6.6 together.
- `isHidden` accepts `{ value, animation }`: in a stack the row grows or shrinks (clipped) while it fades, and the rows below move on the same curve; elsewhere it cross-fades. iOS, Android and the web renderer.
- New `view.transformRotation` (for example, to turn a disclosure chevron).

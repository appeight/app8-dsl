Patch release on 0.7.15. All SDKs move to 0.7.16 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1. One new optional key (`pageControl.properties.style`) and two new expression functions; bundles that don't use them render as before.

- Scroll end, iOS ([scroll-view.md](../docs/components/scroll-view.md)): a screen scrolled to its end stays where its bounce settles. In 0.7.12–0.7.15 a wrapping label that hugs its wrapped text (`trailing <=` its container, or in a leading-aligned stack) measured its wrap again on every variable update, also for variables it doesn't show, and was one line tall for a layout pass. With a screen timer ticking, a scroll resting at its end was clamped to that shorter content and jumped by the label's lost lines (30–60 pt). The label now keeps its wrap while its lines still fit its container. Android and the web renderer measure text inside one layout pass and never showed it.
- Page control `style` ([page-control.md](../docs/components/page-control.md)): `automatic` (default), `light` or `dark`. `automatic` follows the appearance the screen renders in, the one themed colors resolve against: a light capsule with dark dots on a light screen, a dark capsule with light dots on a dark one. Before 0.7.16 the capsule was always dark; set `style: "dark"` to keep that look on a light screen. Explicit dot colors still win. iOS, Android and web.
- `startOfWeek(date)` and `weekdayIndex(date)` ([variables.md](../docs/variables.md)): the first day of the week containing `date`, and the day's index in its week (0 = the first weekday), following the device region's first weekday (Monday in most of Europe, Sunday in the US). A week strip is `addDays(startOfWeek(today()), item)` for `item` 0–6, with today at `weekdayIndex(today())`. iOS, Android and web.

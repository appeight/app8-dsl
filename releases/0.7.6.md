Patch release on 0.7.5. All SDKs move to 0.7.6 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1.

- Video ([video.md](../docs/components/video.md)): `type` is optional on a video and on its posters, inferred from the keys as for `image` (a `name`, `id` or `url` is a `remoteAsset`; a poster with only a `url` is `url`, with only a `time` a `frameAtTime`, else the first frame). iOS and Android required it and drew nothing for a video the web renderer played. A `localAsset` video or poster that is not in the app target resolves through the host's asset map, and a video `url` that is not absolute is an asset name, as for images. The web renderer now fires the playback events (`onVideoReady`, `onVideoStart`, …) like the native players, so a launch screen can wait until its video plays.
- Sheets (iOS 26): after a drag from the medium to the large detent, the screen's bottom bar sits above the home indicator; it stayed in the display's rounded corner until the next relayout.
- Android: a native zoom (`preset: zoom` with a `sourceKey`) pushes, with a back arrow, unless the action sets `mode: modal`, as on iOS; a collection inside a scroll view no longer takes the safe area twice; icon-only bar buttons have accessibility labels; one failing computed variable is dropped and logged instead of replacing the screen with an error ([platforms.md](../docs/platforms.md)).

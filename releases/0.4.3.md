Patch release of the iOS + Android train (spec: https://github.com/appeight/app8-dsl/releases/tag/0.4.3).
- iOS: a reused collection cell is measured at its new item's size. Item templates sized by expressions (`layout.height: "{{item.height}}"`, `isHidden`) kept the previous item's values after reuse, so a short section could sit in a tall cell and leave a blank gap (Facetheory shop after scrolling up and down).
- iOS: `navigationBar.search` with `fillsBar` centres the field 10pt below the status bar, with a matching content inset.
- iOS: labels avoid a lone last word with the `.pushOut` line break strategy, also when a `lineHeight` adds a paragraph style (which used to reset it).
- Android: text uses `LineBreak.Paragraph`, the equivalent of iOS `.pushOut`.
- Docs: `design-best-practices.md`, rules for generating store apps.

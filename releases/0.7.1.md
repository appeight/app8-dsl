Patch release on 0.7.0. All SDKs move to 0.7.1 together (iOS, Android, web renderer).
- Horizontal collections (and horizontal sections of a mixed collection) show no scroll indicator unless `showsScrollIndicator: true`; vertical lists keep theirs. iOS also applies it to the scroll views UIKit embeds for orthogonal sections.
- Adaptive glass: glass (`glass: "normal"`, the tab bar) follows the content behind it, dark over dark content and light over light; `style.glassAppearance: "auto" | "light" | "dark"` (tab bar: `content.style.glassAppearance`) pins it. Clear glass keeps its look.
- `localAsset` names resolve through the app's asset map (the host's `getAsset(assetName:)`) first, then an image compiled into the host app (iOS `UIImage(named:)`, Android a drawable), else nothing.
- Native zoom:
  - iOS: a vertical drag over a scroll view scrolls it; only a pull down at its top (or the leading-edge swipe) starts the interactive dismissal.
  - iOS: a source that fills the presenting screen (a full-screen pager page's photo) stays visible while the incoming screen shrinks back into it (no black screen behind).
  - Android: the moving photo cross-dissolves from the source's image into the destination's (no jump when they differ).
- iOS layout:
  - A multi-line label measured before its container had a width (a card sized from its container) wraps at the real width instead of a few points (text no longer draws outside a card).
  - A label just over one line wraps instead of ending in an ellipsis.
  - Images the system does not prepare for display (8-bit palette PNGs) show.
- Android: public `GlassAppearance` enum and `ViewStyle.glassAppearance` (engine-core API).

Patch release on 0.7.7. All SDKs move to 0.7.8 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1.

- Tab bar styles ([tab-bar-screen.md](../docs/components/tab-bar-screen.md#bar-style), `tabBarScreen.content.barStyle`): a text bar (labels only, no icons) whose selection is a sliding capsule, underline or box, or an opacity cross-fade, with the app's own text styles, background and indicator colour; or a component template that draws the whole bar, with `tabs`, `selectedTab`, `selectedIndex` and an animated `selectionProgress` in scope. The bar keeps its place, the bottom safe area, hide-on-push, badges, the selection haptic and tab accessibility. Without `barStyle` the platform bar is drawn as before. On iOS the bar keeps the system height (`height` is ignored). `selectTab` resolves `tabId` expressions (`{{item.id}}`), and a new `tab` accessibility trait marks custom bar items; unknown trait names are ignored instead of failing the component. iOS, Android and the web renderer.
- Android: Material motion and press feedback. Tab switches fade through instead of cutting. Tappable views and collection cells show a bounded ripple, and a DSL touchDown state replaces it. Pushed screens are prepared before the shared-axis transition, so there is no blank frame. Full-screen covers slide out and sheet scrims fade on dismiss. The predictive-back preview scales to 90% with rounded corners, and the tab bar follows the gesture. iOS is unchanged.

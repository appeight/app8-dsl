Patch release on 0.7.8. All SDKs move to 0.7.9 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1.

- Tab bar selection colour ([tab-bar-screen.md](../docs/components/tab-bar-screen.md#selection-indicator-colour), `tabBarScreen.content.selectionIndicatorColor`): the platform bar's selected-item highlight takes any colour, alpha included: the iOS 26 glass bar's selection pill, the Android Material 3 indicator pill and the web bar in iOS and Android modes. Without it the highlight is black at 10% on a light bar and white at 10% on a dark one (Android and the web drew the tint at 14% before; iOS drew the system grey). `barStyle` bars keep their `indicatorColor`.
- Items sized to content ([collections.md](../docs/collections.md#items-sized-to-content)): `itemWidth: "auto"` on a horizontal collection sizes each item to its content, on one line, so chips hug their label and insets. A horizontal `stackView` in a horizontal `scrollView` takes its width from its arranged views on the web renderer too; it no longer stretches the last one ([scroll-view.md](../docs/components/scroll-view.md#content-size)).
- Edges on a centre ([layout.md](../docs/layout.md#attribute-pairs)): `leading`/`trailing` constraints with `attribute: centerX`, and `top`/`bottom` with `attribute: centerY`, relate to the target's centre on iOS, Android and the web renderer (they related like to like before).
- Repeating animations ([animations.md](../docs/animations.md#repeating-animations)): a per-property `animation` with `options: ["repeat"]` loops from the property's start value to its value for as long as the view is on screen, also after cell reuse, scrolling away and back, a tab switch, a sheet over it and the app returning from the background. It starts on the first apply too (from the rest value). A rotation animates its angle, so a full turn (`-360`) spins; on iOS a full turn also spins without `repeat`. The web renderer played `repeat` once before.
- Web renderer: a screen's `content.android` override (for example its `navigationBar`, a sheet's header order) applies in Android mode ([platforms.md](../docs/platforms.md#per-node-platform-overrides)).

Patch release on 0.7.4. All SDKs move to 0.7.5 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1. The fixes are iOS-only unless noted; Android already behaved this way.

- Images ([image.md](../docs/components/image.md)): a `localAsset` name is an expression like any text. `{{item.image}}` in a collection cell now resolves in the cell's scope; iOS used to look up the literal template and draw nothing.
- Navigation bar ([navigation.md](../docs/navigation.md)): `backLabel` shows its back button only on a pushed screen. The root of a stack (a tab's first screen, a flow's start screen) has none. iOS and the web renderer drew one that went nowhere.
- Hit-testing: a component's children laid out outside its bounds (no clipping) take taps, as on the web and Android. A container whose height resolved to 0 drew its children, but on iOS they were dead.
- Collections: a touch that stops a decelerating or rubber-banding parent scroll view (released after a pull and touched again while it bounces back) only stops it; it no longer also selects the item under the finger. This is iOS-only (UIKit nested scroll views).
- iOS 26 top scroll-edge effect (`topEdgeEffect`): it fades into the screen's `material` fill, and its blur and tint follow the content under the bar, dark over a dark section and light over a light one, like the system effect. A dark screen in a light app used to get a white band.

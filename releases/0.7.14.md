Patch release on 0.7.13. All SDKs move to 0.7.14 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1. No new keys. Two behaviours change: the stretchy top and the native zoom into a photo viewer.

- Stretchy top ([scroll-view.md](../docs/components/scroll-view.md#stretchy-top)): on a pull of `d` the hero's frame grows by `d` with its top on the scroll view's top edge, and only its background scales, by `(h + d) / h` about its bottom centre. The background is the hero's material and every child that covers the whole hero (a photo, a video, a pager). Its other children (buttons, labels, page controls, text blocks) keep their constraints in the taller frame and are never scaled: one pinned to the bottom moves down at its own size, one pinned to the top stays there. Before, the whole hero scaled, buttons and labels included. iOS and the web renderer do this now; Android still scales the whole hero and follows in a later release.
- Stretchy top on iOS: the hero keeps its rest height during the pull. A hero whose height came from content pinned to the safe area (`height >= width` plus a text block on the safe area) used to shrink as fast as it was pulled, so the pull did nothing. A leaf pinned to a view's top (an image, a video, a pager) is that view's background, so the hero is the view.
- Zoom into a photo viewer, iOS ([transitions.md](../docs/transitions.md)): with no `alignmentKey`, the zoom lines up with the incoming element that has the source's key when both show a photo. An image drawn aspect-fit lines up by the picture it draws, cut to the source's shape, so the photo moves as one piece between a card and a viewer with no size jump and no bars. The viewer's floating controls (close button, page counter) keep their size and fade instead of scaling with the screen, also during a drag to dismiss. The web renderer already behaves this way; Android is unchanged.

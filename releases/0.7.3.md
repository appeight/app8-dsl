Patch release on 0.7.2. All SDKs move to 0.7.3 together (iOS, Android, web renderer). `DSL_VERSION` stays 1.1.
- Bar items ([navigation.md](../docs/navigation.md#bar-action)): `icon`, `label` and `color` of `leftAction`, `rightAction` and `rightActions` are expressions in the screen's scope, like `title`, and update when their variables change (a bar heart that follows a wishlist). iOS updates the `UIBarButtonItem` in place with the SF Symbol replace transition (no flicker, Liquid Glass group kept); Android recomposes the top app bar item; the web renderer in both modes. A bar item's `action` may be an array, run in order (iOS and Android rejected the screen before).

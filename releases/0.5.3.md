Patch release on 0.5.2 (spec: https://github.com/appeight/app8-dsl/releases/tag/0.5.0). All SDKs move to 0.5.3 together.
- iOS: zoom-presented screens (product pages, full-screen photo viewers) give horizontal carousels priority over the interactive pop/dismiss. The innermost horizontal scroller keeps a drag while it is moving, for forward drags, and for back drags with content that way; only a back drag on a scroller at rest on its first page (or a leading-edge swipe) pops. Matches native zoom push. When the dismiss begins, the scroller's pan is cancelled so one drag never drives both. Fixes back drags on page 2+ or after a fling popping the screen, and a pinch-zoomed photo dismissing on vertical pans.

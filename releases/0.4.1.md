Patch release of the iOS + Android train (spec: https://github.com/appeight/app8-dsl/releases/tag/0.4.1).
- iOS: horizontal collections and scroll views nested in collection cells keep their scroll offset when the outer cell is reused (e.g. product shelves in a vertical shop list). Offsets are kept per outer item and never leak to another item.
- Android: system chrome matches current Material 3 and Google's own apps: 64dp short navigation bar, top app bar that turns solid on scroll, search bar spacing per spec, shared-axis screen transitions and predictive back, content under a transparent top bar with buttons on circles, `navigationBar.topAppBar` styles (small, centerAligned, medium, large).
- Spec: Android top app bar styles, scroll color, search bar, navigation bar and transitions documented in docs/platforms.md.
